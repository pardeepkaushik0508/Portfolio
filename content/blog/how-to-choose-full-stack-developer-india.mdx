## “Best” should mean evidence—not a slogan

Searches for **best full stack developer in India** or **top full stack developers in India** are commercial. Buyers want someone who will not drop work between design, APIs and hosting.

Empty claims like “#1 developer” are not useful signals. What matters is whether a candidate can show production work, explain trade-offs and own the seams of a project.

This guide explains selection criteria you can verify. It also shows how [Pardeep Kaushik](/about)—a [full stack developer in India](/full-stack-developer-india)—maps to those criteria through public portfolio work.

## Criteria for choosing a full stack developer in India

### 1. Portfolio that proves end-to-end ownership

Ask for projects where the same person touched UI, data/API and release—not only a homepage mockup.

Examples on this site:

- [UtilityTools](/work/utilitytools) — Next.js/React tools platform with Node.js APIs, MongoDB and VPS deployment
- [Aivoxa Labs](/work/aivoxa-labs) — company site with performance work and production hosting
- [YogiSpeaks](/work/yogispeaks) — education-style product architecture with NestJS and PostgreSQL (public site may be offline while hosting changes)

Related reading: [When Should a Business Hire a Full Stack Developer?](/blog/when-hire-full-stack-developer).

### 2. Stack fit for your problem

A strong full stack developer recommends WordPress or Shopify when those platforms are enough—and custom React/Next.js when they are not.

Pardeep’s work spans:

- **Custom / product:** React, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, MongoDB, PostgreSQL
- **WordPress:** Elementor, WooCommerce, migrations and speed work — see [WordPress developer in India](/wordpress-developer-india)
- **Shopify:** Liquid, Shopify 2.0 sections and storefronts — see [Shopify developer in India](/shopify-developer-india)

### 3. Communication without agency ping-pong

For many SMBs and founders, the risk is fragmented freelancers. Prefer a developer who can explain decisions in plain language and keep one backlog.

### 4. Staging before production

Ask how review environments work. Shipping only to live is a red flag for anything beyond a tiny brochure site.

### 5. Performance and responsive delivery

Mobile layout and Core Web Vitals affect leads. Ask how images, caching and template weight are handled. Service overview: [website speed optimization](/website-speed-optimization).

### 6. Deployment literacy

If your team cannot own Nginx, SSL or process managers, hire someone who can. See [VPS deployment for Next.js and Node.js](/blog/vps-deployment-nextjs-nodejs).

## Red flags

- Guaranteed “#1 ranking” or “best developer in India” as a personal title
- No live URLs or case studies
- Cannot explain auth, API contracts or hosting
- Refuses staging
- Keyword-stuffed profiles with no technical depth

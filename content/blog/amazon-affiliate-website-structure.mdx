## What good delivery looks like

A strong **amazon affiliate** engagement starts with audience, offers and constraints—not theme shopping. Define pages, integrations, content ownership and performance targets before development begins.

### Scope questions to answer early

- Who is the primary visitor and what should they do first?
- Which pages are money pages versus support pages?
- Do you need **wordpress website**, ongoing edits, or both?
- What does “done” mean for mobile speed and form reliability?

## Cannibalization rules

1. **Global vs India:** `/wordpress-developer` must not primarily target “WordPress Developer India”. That intent belongs on `/wordpress-developer-india` (same pattern for Shopify and Full Stack).
2. **Generic vs specialist speed:** `/website-speed-optimization` = umbrella; `/wordpress-speed-optimization` and `/shopify-speed-optimization` = platform-specific.
3. **Liquid vs Theme:** `/shopify-liquid-developer` = Liquid sections/templates; `/shopify-theme-development` = full theme architecture / OS 2.0 systems. Keep both only while content stays distinct.
4. **Blog vs money pages:** Hiring/cost guides stay informational on `/blog/*` and link to commercial landings—do not duplicate money-page H1s.
5. **No doorway pages:** Do not create hire-/best-/freelance- duplicate slugs for the same India or global cluster.
6. **No country doorways yet:** Strengthen `/remote-web-developer` before USA/UK/AU/CA pages.

## CTA copy (money pages)

- Primary: **Get a Free Project Estimate** → `/contact`
- Secondary: **View My Work** / **View Related Projects** → `/#work` or related case studies
